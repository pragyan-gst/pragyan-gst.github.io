Headline — say what changed, not what the article is about

Two sentences. What changed, and the one thing a reader will get wrong if they stop reading here.

First heading

Body text. Cite the provision or notification for every figure — Notification No. NN/YYYY-Central Tax (Rate) dated DD.MM.YYYY, Section NN, Rule NN. A figure without its source cannot be used in a notice or a reply.

Use a warn box for the thing most likely to be got backwards.

Use an info box for a caution that qualifies rather than corrects — a provision that overrides the obvious reading.

Second heading

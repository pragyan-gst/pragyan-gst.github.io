Reporting a wrong figure?

The most useful report includes:

  • the page, chart or calculator;
  • the financial year or tax period;
  • the figure shown and the figure you expected;
  • the provision, notification or source you relied on, if known.

Please do not send real taxpayer data. Do not attach returns, invoices, GSTINs or taxpayer names. A description is normally enough for us to investigate a problem.

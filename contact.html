What is worth writing about

Something is wrong. A figure, a date, a citation, a broken link. This is the most useful mail I get. If you can say which page and what you expected instead, better still.

You want the desktop tool. Say so and I will send the current build and the release notes. It is a Windows application and it is free.

Something is missing. A chart that would help, a return the tool should read, a check that would be useful. No promises on timing, but it goes on the list.

You are stuck with the tool. Include the version number from the title bar and, if you can, what you were doing when it went wrong.

What I cannot help with

I cannot advise on your own case, your client's case, or a notice you have received. Not being coy — it would be improper, and a stranger who has not seen your record is the wrong person to ask. A practitioner who can see the papers is the right one.

Please do not send returns, notices or anything with a GSTIN on it. I do not want other people's tax records in my inbox, and I have no way to keep them safely. If a problem needs data to reproduce, strip the identifiers first or describe the shape of it instead.

Reply times

This is not anyone's job. I read everything and usually reply within a few days. If something is broken and you are on a deadline, say so in the subject line.

About GST Pragyan

Built for the person who has to actually work with GST.

GST Pragyan started with a simple idea: GST information should help you answer a question, not make you search through ten pages before you can start.

Why we built it

Less hunting. More understanding.

GST work often comes down to a handful of practical questions: what applies, from which date, how much, and whether something is still within time. Pragyan brings those questions into one calm workspace.

01

Start with the question

Need a due date? A late-fee calculation? A limitation date? A historical rate? Go directly to the relevant working page instead of navigating a maze.

02

See the rule behind the number

Figures are presented with their statutory basis and the relevant period. The aim is to make the reasoning visible, not just display a result.

03

Use the result as working material

The reference pages and calculators are aids to analysis. They do not replace checking the facts, the applicable law or the notifications in force for the period.

Made for real work

One place, different users.

Pragyan is designed to be useful whether you are checking your own compliance, preparing a professional opinion, reviewing a case or learning how the GST framework fits together.

↗

Learners

Understand the timeline and logic behind GST provisions instead of memorising isolated numbers.

01

Clarity over clutter

Pages are organised around tasks. Important information gets room; secondary detail stays available without taking over the screen.

02

History matters

A number that is correct today may be wrong for an older tax period. Pragyan therefore treats dates and changes as first-class information.

03

Show the limits

Where a result needs verification or depends on an assumption, it should say so. A useful tool should make uncertainty visible.

04

Privacy by design

The desktop workflow is designed around local processing. The data you work with should remain under your control.

A practical boundary

Pragyan is a working aid, not an authority.

Use it to organise thinking, perform calculations and locate the relevant provision. Before relying on an answer in a live matter, verify the facts and the law applicable to that specific period.

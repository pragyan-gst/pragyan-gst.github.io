Privacy

Your data stays where you put it.

The application runs on your computer and keeps your data there. This website collects nothing about you. That is the whole policy; the rest is detail.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

In one paragraph. The GST Pragyan desktop application runs entirely on your own computer. The returns you load into it, the workbooks it writes and the drafts it produces never leave that machine. There is no account, no sign-in, no licence check and no telemetry. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from a third party except the two web fonts named below. If you email us, we hold that email and nothing more.

1. Who this is from

GST Pragyan is an independent working aid for GST return scrutiny, published by its author and contactable at pragyan.gst@gmail.com. It is not published by, endorsed by or affiliated with the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, the GST Network, or any Government of India body.

This policy covers two separate things \u2014 the desktop application and this website \u2014 addressed separately, because they behave quite differently.

2. The desktop application

2.1 What it processes

You give it files you have downloaded from the GST portal or prepared yourself. These typically contain a taxpayer\u2019s GSTIN, trade name, invoice particulars, supplier and recipient details and tax figures. Under Indian data protection law such information may be personal data, and where it is handled in the course of tax administration it is also official information.

2.2 Where that data goes

Nowhere. Pragyan reads the files you point it at, writes its workbooks and drafts to a folder you choose, and does nothing else. Specifically, it:

  • does not upload returns, invoices, GSTINs, trade names or any figure to us or to any server;
  • does not require an account, sign-in, licence key or activation;
  • does not send usage statistics, crash reports or telemetry;
  • does not record who is using it \u2014 no name, office, file number or case register is collected, because none of that is needed to reconcile a return and an office\u2019s own records are where such things belong;
  • does not need an internet connection to do any of its work.

The consequence is worth stating plainly: we never see your data, and we could not produce it if we were asked to.

2.3 What stays on your computer

Pragyan writes the workbooks and drafts you ask for, where you choose. It may keep small local files such as your last-used folder. All of it sits on your machine and is removed when you delete it. You remain responsible for that data \u2014 where you store it, who can reach it, and the departmental instructions that govern it.

2.4 Security

Because nothing is transmitted, the security of the data is the security of the computer it is on. Where the data is official, follow the instructions that govern it: keep the machine under your control, use full-disk encryption where your department provides it, and do not place workbooks containing taxpayer particulars on personal cloud storage or unencrypted removable media.

3. This website

3.1 What it collects

Nothing. These are static pages with:

  • no cookies of any kind, including analytics or preference cookies;
  • no analytics \u2014 no Google Analytics, no pixel, no tag manager;
  • no advertising and no tracking scripts;
  • no contact form, comments, or newsletter sign-up;
  • no login and no user accounts.

3.2 Web fonts

The pages load two typefaces \u2014 Space Grotesk and IBM Plex \u2014 from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore makes a request to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which reveals your IP address to Google in the same way visiting any page with a third-party asset does. No cookie is set by that request. If you would rather avoid it, the fonts can be self-hosted; write to us and we will do it.

3.3 The calculator

The late fee and interest calculator runs entirely in your browser using a small script served from this site. The dates and figures you type are never sent anywhere and are not stored \u2014 close the tab and they are gone.

3.4 Server logs

This site is served by a third-party static host. Like any web server, that host may keep short-lived technical logs \u2014 IP address, time, page requested, user-agent \u2014 for security and to keep the service running. We do not control those logs, do not analyse them, and do not combine them with anything else.

4. If you email us

We receive your email address, your message and whatever you choose to include. We use it to reply and to fix what you report \u2014 nothing else. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not pass your address on. The mailbox is hosted by Google, so Google\u2019s terms apply to mail in transit and at rest. Ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

Please do not email us real return data. If you are reporting a wrong figure, tell us the parameter number, the tax period, the figure Pragyan produced and the figure you expected. That is almost always enough. Do not attach returns, invoices, GSTINs or taxpayer names \u2014 sending official taxpayer information to a third party may also breach your own departmental instructions.

5. Children

Pragyan is a professional tool for tax compliance and administration and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under eighteen.

6. Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights over personal data held about you. In our case the answer is short: the only personal data we hold is the email correspondence you have sent us, because the application transmits nothing and the website collects nothing. Write to us and we will tell you what we have, or delete it.

Where Pragyan is used on official data in the course of duty, the department \u2014 not us \u2014 is the controller of that data.

7. Links to other sites

Where this site links anywhere else, those sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.

8. Changes

A revised policy will be published here with a new date at the top. If a change ever means the application or the site begins collecting something \u2014 which is not planned \u2014 it will be stated prominently and not buried in a revision.
